01 / Identity & Philosophy

Engineering AI into reliable software.

I am a final-year CS (Data Science) student at Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology, Mangalore. My focus is turning machine learning algorithms and intelligent capabilities into robust, inspectable production software systems.

Who I Am & What I Build

Most machine learning education stops where the real engineering challenge begins: inside exploratory Jupyter notebooks with hardcoded paths, uncontrolled data leakage, and zero consideration for production latency, security, or error recovery.

My trajectory as an AI Engineer & Software Developer is defined by bridging this divide. When I train an XGBoost model, I don't just inspect validation curves—I eliminate target leakage columns, wrap it in a typed FastAPI microservice, track training runs with MLflow, and package it into a reproducible Docker container deployed to the cloud.

"Builds AI-powered systems and honest software: ML paired with real interfaces, not just notebooks."

Beyond applied AI, I dive deep into low-level systems. My empirical research in C11 and OpenMP demonstrated that restructuring loop memory strides (ikj over ijk) delivers an immediate ~12.6x speedup by maximizing hardware L1/L2 cache locality—proving that hardware empathy matters just as much as throwing more compute at an algorithm.

In full-stack engineering, I architected the security layer for AIET-UniSphere, our campus platform for Alva's Institute. By baking authorization rules directly into PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies evaluated on authenticated JWT sessions, we eliminated an entire class of API authorization bypass bugs without needing custom backend server proxies.

Target Roles & Availability

I am actively interviewing for AI Engineer, AI Developer, Applied ML, and Full-Stack Systems internships starting from December 2026. I bring clean coding discipline, reproducible benchmarking rigor, and an uncompromising attitude toward honest technical communication.
